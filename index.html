The "Two Leaves and a Bud" Standard

In the premium tea industry, "two leaves and a bud" represents the traditional standard for quality pluck. We harvest only the top two youngest leaves and the tender unopened bud of the tea plant (Camellia sinensis) to yield tea that is sweetest, least bitter, and full of complex aromatics.

By using full, whole tea leaves instead of broken bits and tea dust, we preserve the essential flavor oils. Our pyramid-shaped sachets are fully compostable, plant-based, and specifically engineered to give the leaves room to unfurl and steep cleanly.

Whole Leaf Only

No dust or fannings. You get full, premium tea leaves in every sachet.

Compostable Sachets

Made from plant-based renewable resources, fully biodegradable.

100% Organic

Certified organic farming. No artificial flavorings, chemicals, or pesticides.

Fair Sourcing

We work directly with growers to ensure fair trade and community support.
